## Design direction

**Concept: the manifest.** The site reads like a well-run shipping document: warm paper background, ink-navy text, container-orange accents, thin grid lines like a pallet floor plan, and small monospace labels like those on a waybill. It looks like logistics without stock photos of ships or cranes, and it will not be mistaken for a template.

### Palette (all pairs checked for WCAG AA contrast)

| Role | Light | Dark | Contrast on background |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Background | #F4F1EA (paper) | #0B1220 | n/a |
| Text | #0F1B2D (ink) | #EDE9DF | 15.3:1 light, 15.4:1 dark |
| Secondary text | #4B5B6E | #9AA8BA | 6.2:1 light, 7.7:1 dark |
| Accent (buttons, links) | #C2410C (container orange) | #FB923C | 4.6:1 light, 8.3:1 dark; white text on the orange button is 5.2:1 |
| Success / "cleared" | #0B6B5F (teal) | #2DD4BF | 5.7:1 light, 10.1:1 dark |

Use orange sparingly: one primary button per screen, the active timeline node, and the big numbers. Use teal only for "cleared / on time / accurate" status chips.

### Typography

- **Headings and big numbers:** a strong grotesque such as Archivo or Barlow Condensed, tight line height, large scale.
- **Body:** IBM Plex Sans at 17 to 18 px, 1.6 line height.
- **Labels and tags:** IBM Plex Mono in small caps, for things like "CLEARED · 0 HOLDS" and "JAFZA → OPEN TO WORK".
- Load through `next/font` so there is no layout shift.

### Signature elements

- **Waybill header strip** above the hero: a monospace line with your name, base and status, e.g. `SATTANATHAN CHANDRAN · DXB / JAFZA · STATUS: OPEN TO ASSISTANT MANAGER ROLES`.
- **Stamp chips** beside key results: CLEARED, ON TIME, ACCURATE. Rotate them slightly (about 2 degrees) for a stamped feel, but keep the text horizontal for screen readers.
- **Route line:** a dashed line that draws itself down the experience timeline, with nodes for each role.
- **Sticker-style counters** for the six proof numbers, with a subtle grid behind them.
- **Section numbering** in mono (01 Hero, 02 Proof, 03 About) to reinforce the document feel.

### Motion and interaction

- Animate only on first scroll into view: counters, route line, stamp "press". Each under 800 ms.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by showing final values immediately.
- Skip the heavy 3D globe from your earlier HTML portfolio. It costs load time on a recruiter's phone and adds little proof. If you want a map, use a light static SVG of the UAE with a JAFZA pin.

### Layout rules

- Mobile first: most recruiters will open the link from LinkedIn or WhatsApp on a phone. On mobile, show a bottom bar with CV and Contact.
- Max content width 1120 px, 8 px spacing scale, one idea per screen.
- Light theme by default, dark theme following the visitor's system setting, with a visible toggle.
- Accessibility: visible focus rings, semantic headings, alt text on any image, buttons at least 44 px tall, links that describe their destination.
- Photo: one professional headshot in the About section is worth including if you have one; it makes the site feel real. Avoid stock imagery.

## Build and SEO notes

Stay on the stack you already run at satteches.com (Next.js on Vercel with Cloudflare) so deployment and your domain keep working.

- **Stack:** Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion for the few animations, `next/font`. Content lives in one `content.ts` (or JSON) file so you can edit copy without touching components.
- **Performance targets:** Lighthouse 95+ on mobile for Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices and SEO. Largest paint under 2.5 s, no layout shift, total JavaScript kept small.
- **Search terms to work into titles and headings naturally:** logistics coordinator Dubai, freight forwarding JAFZA, Mirsal 2 customs clearance, SAP MM WM inventory controller, 3PL warehouse operations UAE.
- **Page title:** Sattanathan Chandran | Logistics Coordinator, Dubai (Freight, Customs, SAP)
- **Meta description:** Logistics coordinator in JAFZA, Dubai. 99.8% inventory accuracy, zero customs holds on Mirsal 2, SAP S/4HANA, and self-built automation. Open to Assistant Manager roles.
- **Structured data:** JSON-LD `Person` schema with job title, employer, location, `sameAs` links (LinkedIn, GitHub) and `knowsAbout`.
- **Social preview:** a generated Open Graph image (1200 × 630) showing your name, role and three numbers. This is what appears when the link is pasted into LinkedIn or WhatsApp, so it matters.
- **Contact form:** server action sending email through Resend or similar, with spam protection (honeypot plus rate limit). Also keep a plain `mailto:` link as fallback.
- **CV file:** one PDF, named `Sattanathan-Chandran-CV.pdf`, served from the site. Keep it identical to the /cv page.
- **Analytics:** Plausible, Vercel Web Analytics or similar. Track CV downloads, contact clicks and `/for/...` link visits as events.
- **Privacy:** your phone number and email are in the CV. For the public page, show the email and hide the phone behind a click-to-reveal or a WhatsApp button, to reduce spam and scraping. Do not publish your home address or visa document details.
- **Housekeeping:** `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt`, custom 404 page in the same manifest style, and a favicon with your initials.
